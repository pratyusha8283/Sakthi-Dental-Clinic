01

Information We Collect

We may collect personal identification information from users in several ways, including when users visit our site, fill out form requests (such as scheduling appointments or general inquiries), and engage with clinic resources.

Personal Information Provided Voluntarily:

  • Full Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number & Appointment Preferences

Users can visit our site anonymously. We only collect personal data when it is voluntarily submitted. Refusing to provide personal details may limit access to specific website features or online appointment booking.

Non-Personal Technical Data:

  • Browser type and version
  • Device type (Mobile, Tablet, Desktop)
  • Operating system and ISP information
02

Use of Cookies

Our website uses "cookies" to enhance visitor browsing experience. Cookies are small data files stored on your device that help us analyze web traffic, remember user preferences, and optimize site responsiveness.

You can set your web browser to refuse cookies or alert you when cookies are being sent. Note that disabling cookies may cause some sections of the website to function with limited interactivity.

03

How We Use Your Information

Sakthi Dental Clinic uses collected information for legitimate clinical and organizational purposes:

  • To Manage Appointments & Inquiries: Direct communication regarding consultation schedules, treatments, and follow-ups.
  • To Improve Patient Service: Feedback helps us respond faster to patient requests and emergency support needs.
  • To Optimize Website Functionality: Monitoring general user interaction to enhance site performance and accessibility.
  • Email & SMS Updates: Sending confirmation messages, reminders, or response letters regarding your dental inquiries.
04

Data Security Practices

We implement strict administrative, technical, and physical security procedures during data collection, storage, and processing. This protects your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

05

Information Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, or rent personal identification information to third parties under any circumstances.

We may share generic, aggregated demographic information (not linked to any individual identity) with trusted analytics partners solely for the purpose of maintaining website quality and performance.

06

Children's Online Privacy (COPPA)

In compliance with international standards and COPPA regulations, our website does not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13 years of age. Pediatric consultations must be arranged directly by a parent or legal guardian.

07

Changes to This Policy

Sakthi Dental Clinic reserves the right to update this Privacy Policy periodically. Whenever changes are made, the revised date at the top of this page will be updated. We encourage patients to review this page occasionally to stay informed.

08

Acceptance of Terms

By browsing and utilizing this website, you signify your acceptance of this policy. If you do not agree to this Privacy Policy, please discontinue site usage. Continued use following posted updates constitutes acceptance of those changes.
